# DRAFT - for attorney review

**Ethics, Supervision and Outsourcing Policy (draft v0.1)**

> This is a working draft prepared by the project integrator so that counsel has a structure to review. It is **not legal advice**, makes no statement about what any rule requires, and must not be relied on or published until a licensed attorney has reviewed and approved it. Every item marked **[Counsel to confirm]** is an open question, not a position.

Related SOW items: 3B.1, 4.2, 11.4, Annex B "Ethics & Compliance Policy Pack".

## 1. Purpose

To describe how CareHomesSupportDocs.org ("the Organization") and its legal process outsourcing (LPO) support team work under the direction and supervision of licensed partner attorneys, so that the supervising attorneys can meet their professional obligations.

The SOW names ABA Model Rule 5.3 (responsibilities regarding nonlawyer assistance) and the California analogs as the reference points. **[Counsel to confirm]** which California Rules of Professional Conduct, State Bar opinions and other authorities apply, and how this policy must reflect them.

## 2. Scope

- People: partner attorneys; Organization staff and volunteers; the Philippines-based LPO team; moderators and administrators of the web platform.
- Work: drafting, formatting, research support, document preparation and quality checks carried out under attorney instruction.
- Out of scope: anything that is the practice of law by a nonlawyer. **[Counsel to define]** the boundary for this program.

## 3. Roles

| Role | Responsibilities (draft) |
|---|---|
| Partner attorney | Accepts the matter; gives instructions; reviews every draft; approves, signs and authorizes filing or delivery. **[Counsel to confirm]** |
| LPO team member | Drafts only under written attorney instruction; follows the LPO workflow SOP (`docs/lpo-workflow-sop.md`); never gives advice to members |
| Organization staff / moderators | Intake, triage, scheduling, platform moderation; never give legal advice |
| Program lead | Keeps training records, access lists and this policy current |

## 4. Supervision controls (to be confirmed by counsel)

1. **Written instruction.** No drafting starts without a written instruction from the assigned partner attorney.
2. **Draft marking.** Drafts stay marked "FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW ONLY" until the attorney approves them. In this project, drafting happens outside the web platform; the platform does not apply this mark (see `docs/CHANGE_ORDER_DRAFT.md`).
3. **Attorney review.** Every draft is reviewed by the partner attorney before it leaves the team. Revisions follow the redline/approval cycle in the LPO SOP.
4. **No direct advice.** LPO staff and Organization staff do not communicate legal conclusions to members.
5. **Records.** Instruction, review and approval are recorded per matter. **[Counsel to confirm]** what record is required and for how long.
6. **Training.** Every team member completes onboarding on confidentiality, this policy and the LPO SOP before working on matters. Training is recorded.
7. **Escalation.** Any question that may involve legal judgment goes to the partner attorney.
