# DRAFT - for attorney review

**Conflicts Check Protocol (draft v0.1)**

> Working draft prepared by the project integrator for counsel to review. It is **not legal advice** and takes no position on what any rule requires. Items marked **[Counsel to confirm]** are open questions. Do not use or publish it until a licensed attorney has approved it.

Related SOW items: 3B.2, 4.2, Annex B "Ethics & Compliance Policy Pack".

## 1. Purpose

To give the Organization and its partner attorneys a consistent way to identify potential conflicts of interest before a matter is accepted and while it is open.

## 2. When a check is run (draft)

1. Before a partner attorney accepts a new matter.
2. When a new party, facility or related entity appears in an open matter.
3. When a team member (attorney, LPO staff, moderator) joins a matter.
4. **[Counsel to confirm]** any other trigger.

## 3. Information collected for a check (draft, minimum necessary)

| Item | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Member organization | Organization name from the membership record | Already held by the platform |
| Facility | Facility name and CCLD facility number | Public licensing data |
| Adverse or related parties | Agency office, other parties named in the citation | **[Counsel to confirm]** what counts as an adverse party here |
| Assigned team | Partner attorney, LPO staff, moderator | |

Do **not** collect resident names, health information or other resident-identifying details for a conflicts check. The platform's policy is no PHI (SOW 11.3).

## 4. How the check is done (draft)

1. Intake records the items in section 3.
2. The program lead searches the matter list and the partner attorney's own conflicts system. **[Counsel to confirm]** which system is the record of truth, since the web platform has no conflicts database.
3. Result recorded as: clear / potential conflict / conflict.
4. Potential conflicts go to the partner attorney for a decision. **[Counsel to confirm]** whether waivers are possible and how they are documented.
5. A matter is not assigned until the result is recorded.

## 5. Team member conflicts

Team members disclose any personal or financial relationship with a facility, member or agency office involved in a matter. Disclosures go to the program lead and the partner attorney. **[Counsel to confirm]** what disclosure form is needed.

## 6. Moderators of public content

Platform moderators must not moderate a rebuttal for a facility or member organization they have a relationship with. They recuse and another moderator acts. **[Counsel / client to confirm]**

## 7. Records

Each check is recorded with date, who ran it, sources searched and the result. **[Counsel to confirm]** retention period (see `docs/policies/data-retention-policy.md`).
