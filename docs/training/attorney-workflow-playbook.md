# Attorney Workflow Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook provides guidance for partner attorneys who assist licensed California care facility operators using CareHomesSupportDocs.com. It outlines the recommended workflow for reviewing regulatory citations, advising facility operators, and preparing rebuttals for publication through the platform.

---

## Audience

This document is intended for:

- Partner Attorneys
- Legal Consultants
- Authorized Legal Representatives
- Operations Team

---

## Overview

CareHomesSupportDocs.com is an independent nonprofit platform that enables licensed California care facility operators to publish rebuttals to regulatory citations.

Partner attorneys may assist facilities by reviewing citations, providing legal guidance, and helping prepare professional rebuttals. The platform itself does **not** provide legal advice or legal representation.

---

# Attorney Workflow

## Step 1 – Receive Facility Request

The attorney receives a request for assistance from a participating facility.

The request may include:

- Citation details
- Supporting documentation
- Facility background information
- Draft rebuttal (if available)

---

## Step 2 – Review Citation

Review the available citation information to understand:

- The cited regulation
- Alleged deficiency
- Supporting evidence
- Applicable compliance requirements

If additional information is required, request clarification from the facility before proceeding.

---

## Step 3 – Evaluate the Case

Review all available documentation to determine:

- Whether additional clarification is needed
- Potential factual corrections
- Supporting documentation available from the facility

This review should remain objective and based on the information provided by the facility.

---

## Step 4 – Prepare or Review the Rebuttal

Assist the facility in preparing a professional rebuttal.

Recommended practices include:

- Present factual information
- Reference supporting documentation where appropriate
- Maintain a respectful and professional tone
- Avoid speculative or inflammatory language

---

## Step 5 – Submit Through the Platform

Once finalized, the facility submits the rebuttal through CareHomesSupportDocs.com.

> **Status:** For confirmation.

Attorneys may review the final submission before publication where applicable.

---

## Step 6 – Publication and Follow-Up

After moderation approval, the rebuttal is published on the facility's public profile.

Attorneys may continue supporting the facility by:

- Answering follow-up questions
- Reviewing future citations
- Providing additional legal guidance outside the platform when appropriate

---

# Attorney Responsibilities

Partner attorneys should:

- Maintain confidentiality of client information.
- Provide accurate legal guidance within their professional scope.
- Review supporting documentation carefully.
- Promote factual and professional rebuttal submissions.
- Comply with applicable legal and ethical standards.

---

# Professional Standards

Attorneys should:

- Maintain client confidentiality.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Provide objective legal guidance.
- Communicate professionally with facility representatives.
- Respect platform moderation policies.

---

# Frequently Encountered Issues

### Incomplete Documentation

Request additional documentation before providing legal guidance.

---

### Missing Citation Information

Ask the facility to provide the official citation documentation before proceeding.

---

### Privacy Concerns

Advise the facility to remove any Protected Health Information (PHI) or Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before submission.

---

### Moderation Revisions Requested

Review the moderator's feedback with the facility and assist in preparing an updated rebuttal if necessary.

---

# Best Practices

Partner attorneys are encouraged to:

- Base recommendations on documented facts.
- Keep communications professional and objective.
- Review rebuttals before submission when possible.
- Encourage compliance with platform policies.
- Coordinate with facility representatives throughout the review process.
