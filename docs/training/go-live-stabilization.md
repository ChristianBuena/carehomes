# Go-Live Stabilization Plan

## Purpose

This document outlines the post-launch stabilization plan for CareHomesSupportDocs.com following production deployment. The stabilization period is intended to ensure platform reliability, address operational issues promptly, and support client adoption during the initial rollout.

---

# Objectives

The stabilization period aims to:

- Monitor platform performance after launch.
- Resolve reported issues within agreed service levels.
- Support client leadership, operations staff, and partner attorneys.
- Ensure a smooth transition into normal platform operations.
- Collect feedback for future platform improvements.

---

# Stabilization Period

**Duration**

60 calendar days following production launch.

**Status**

Planned (for confirmation).

---

# Support Coverage

During the stabilization period, the project team will provide support for:

- Platform availability
- User access issues
- Authentication and login problems
- Membership-related issues
- Rebuttal submission workflow
- Moderation process support
- General platform questions

---

# Weekly Stand-Up Meetings

Weekly status meetings will be conducted throughout the stabilization period.

**Attendees**

- Client Leadership
- Operations Team
- Project Team
- Technical Representatives (as needed)

**Agenda**

- Review open issues
- Discuss completed fixes
- Review user feedback
- Identify risks or blockers
- Plan upcoming activities

Meeting schedule and attendees are subject to client confirmation.

---

# Service Level Targets

| Priority | Description | Target Response |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| P1 | Critical platform outage or security incident | Less than 4 business hours |
| P2 | Major functionality affected | Within 1 business day |
| P3 | Minor functionality issue | Within 3 business days |
| P4 | Enhancement request or cosmetic issue | Next planned maintenance cycle |

---

# Issue Management

Reported issues should be:

1. Logged by the project team.
2. Assigned an appropriate priority.
3. Investigated and tracked until resolution.
4. Verified after implementation.
5. Documented for future reference.

---

# Success Criteria

The stabilization period will be considered successful when:

- Critical incidents are resolved within agreed response targets.
- Platform stability is maintained.
- Users are able to complete normal workflows.
- Client feedback is addressed appropriately.
- No major unresolved production issues remain.

---

# Deliverables

The stabilization phase will produce:

- Weekly status updates
- Issue tracking records
- Support documentation
- Final stabilization summary

---

# Risks

Potential risks during stabilization include:

- Unexpected production defects
- User adoption challenges
- Payment or authentication issues
- Configuration errors
- Third-party service interruptions

Risks should be monitored throughout the stabilization period and addressed according to the Incident Response Playbook.
