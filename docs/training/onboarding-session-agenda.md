# Client Onboarding Session Agenda

## Purpose

This session introduces client leadership, operations staff, and partner attorneys to the CareHomesSupportDocs platform. It covers system navigation, user responsibilities, moderation workflow, and support procedures prior to production deployment.

---

# Session Information

**Audience**
- Client Leadership
- Operations Team
- Partner Attorneys

**Estimated Duration**
- 60–90 minutes

**Delivery Method**
- Microsoft Teams / Google Meet (for confirmation)

**Facilitator**
- Project Team (for confirmation)

---

# Agenda

## 1. Welcome & Project Overview (10 minutes)

Topics:
- Project objectives
- Platform capabilities
- User roles
- Expected workflows

---

## 2. System Walkthrough (20 minutes)

Topics:
- Login process
- Dashboard overview
- Facility management
- Rebuttal submission
- Citation tracking
- Public facility directory

---

## 3. Moderation Workflow (10 minutes)

Topics:
- Submission review process
- Privacy requirements
- Approval workflow
- Publishing process

---

## 4. Attorney Workflow (10 minutes)

Topics:
- Reviewing facility rebuttals
- Providing legal guidance
- Compliance considerations
- Communication with facility administrators

---

## 5. Incident Reporting & Support (10 minutes)

Topics:
- Reporting technical issues
- Escalation process
- Priority levels
- Support response expectations

---

## 6. Questions & Answers (10–20 minutes)

Open discussion and clarification of workflows.

---

- Attorney Workflow Playbook

# Expected Outcomes

Participants should be able to:

- Navigate the platform
- Understand their assigned responsibilities
- Submit and review rebuttals
- Follow moderation procedures
- Report incidents appropriately
- Locate documentation within the Knowledge Base
