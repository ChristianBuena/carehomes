# LPO Workflow SOP

## Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the Legal Process Outsourcing (LPO) workflow for preparing legal documents under the supervision of a licensed attorney. It establishes standardized procedures for document intake, drafting, attorney review, approval, filing, and secure record management while ensuring compliance with confidentiality, quality assurance, and applicable professional responsibility requirements.

---

## Scope

This SOP applies to all personnel involved in the preparation and review of legal documents within the CareHomesSupportDocs.org platform, including administrative staff, document drafters, moderators, and supervising attorneys.

The procedures described in this document apply only to draft legal documents prepared under attorney supervision. Final legal advice, approval, and execution remain the responsibility of the supervising licensed attorney.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Member | Upload supporting documents, provide required information, review completed work, and approve document sharing with an attorney. |
| Administrator | Manage intake, verify submissions, maintain document records, and coordinate workflow activities. |
| LPO Drafter | Prepare draft legal documents according to attorney instructions and organizational standards. |
| Supervising Attorney | Review draft documents, provide legal guidance, approve revisions, and authorize final documents before release. |
| System | Securely store documents, maintain version history, control access permissions, and record workflow activity. |

---

## LPO Workflow

### 1. Intake

1. The member submits a request for legal document preparation through the platform.
2. Supporting documents are uploaded to the organization's secure document storage.
3. The system associates all uploaded files with the requesting organization.
4. The member confirms which documents may be shared with the supervising attorney.

---

### 2. Triage

1. An administrator reviews the submission for completeness.
2. Missing or incomplete information is identified.
3. If additional documentation is required, the member is notified before drafting begins.
4. Complete requests are assigned to the drafting stage.

---

### 3. Drafting

1. The assigned drafter prepares the requested legal document using the information provided by the member.
2. Drafts are prepared only under the direction of a supervising attorney.
3. Every draft document must be clearly marked:

**FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW ONLY**

---

4. Draft documents are saved using the organization's naming and versioning standards.

---

### 4. Attorney Review

1. The completed draft is submitted to the supervising attorney for legal review.
2. The attorney verifies the accuracy, completeness, and legal sufficiency of the document.
3. Any required corrections, additions, or legal comments are recorded before the document proceeds to the revision stage.
4. Draft documents remain marked **"FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW ONLY"** until final approval.

---

### 5. Redline and Revisions

1. The drafter reviews the attorney's feedback and updates the document accordingly.
2. All revisions are tracked using the organization's document versioning standards.
3. Updated drafts are resubmitted to the supervising attorney until all requested revisions have been completed.
4. Previous document versions are retained for audit and record-keeping purposes.

---

### 6. Approval

1. After all revisions have been completed, the supervising attorney performs a final review.
2. If approved, the attorney authorizes the document for final preparation.
3. No draft document may be released or distributed without attorney approval.

---

### 7. Signature

1. Where required, the approved document is provided for electronic or physical signature.
2. Members complete any required signatures before the document is finalized.
3. Signed documents are securely stored within the organization's document repository.

---

### 8. Filing

1. Final documents are filed according to organizational record management procedures.
2. Documents are associated with the appropriate organization and matter.
3. Access permissions are applied to ensure only authorized users may view or download the documents.

---

### 9. Close

1. The completed matter is marked as closed after all required documents have been finalized and stored.
2. Members retain access to authorized documents through their secure account.
3. All workflow activities and document history remain available for auditing and compliance purposes.

---

## Naming and Versioning Standards

- Documents should use a consistent naming convention.
- Each revision must include an incremented version number (e.g., v1.0, v1.1, v2.0).
- Draft documents must remain clearly identified until attorney approval is complete.
- Superseded versions should be retained in accordance with the organization's document retention policy.

---

## Quality Assurance Checklist

Before a document is submitted for attorney review, verify that:

- All required supporting documents have been received.
- Member information is complete and accurate.
- Required templates have been used.
- Document formatting is consistent.
- The draft includes the required **"FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW ONLY"** watermark.
- The correct document version has been saved.
- Confidential information has been handled according to organizational policies.

---

## Attorney Supervision Requirements

- All legal document drafting must be performed under the supervision of a licensed attorney.
- LPO personnel may prepare draft documents but may not provide independent legal advice.
- Final legal review and approval remain the responsibility of the supervising attorney.
- Any legal questions outside the drafting process must be referred to the supervising attorney.

---

## Security and Confidentiality

- Documents must be stored in the organization's secure document repository.
- Access to documents is restricted to authorized users based on assigned permissions.
- Members may choose to share all or selected documents with their attorney through the platform.
- Confidential information must be handled in accordance with the organization's privacy, confidentiality, and data retention policies.
- Document access and modifications should be recorded for auditing purposes.
