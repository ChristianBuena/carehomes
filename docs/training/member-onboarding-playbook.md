## Onboarding Process

### Step 1 – Create an Account

Describe:
- Visit the Sign Up page
- Enter account information
- Submit registration

---

### Step 2 – Verify Email Address

>**Status:** For Confirmation

Describe:
- Open verification email
- Click verification link
- Return to the platform

---

### Step 3 – Log In

Describe:
- Enter credentials
- Complete MFA (if enabled)
- Access dashboard

---

### Step 4 – Complete Organization Profile

Describe:
- Organization details
- Facility information
- Contact information

---

### Step 5 – Select a Membership Plan

Describe:
- Available plans
- Annual billing
- Secure payment through Stripe

---

### Step 6 – Account Verification

Describe:
- Manual verification process
- CCLD license verification
- Expected review timeline

---

### Step 7 – Access Member Features

List features available after approval.

Example:

## Best Practices

Provide recommendations for new members.

Example:
- Keep organization information updated.
- Submit factual and professional rebuttals.
- Protect account credentials.
- Review moderation guidelines before submitting content.

---

## Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions.

Example:

### Verification email not received

Possible causes
- Spam folder
- Incorrect email

Resolution
- Check spam folder
- Request another verification email

---

### Unable to log in

Possible causes
- Incorrect password
- Account not yet approved

Resolution
- Reset password
- Contact support

---

### Membership payment issues

Possible causes
- Card declined
- Payment interruption

Resolution
- Retry payment
- Contact Stripe support if necessary
