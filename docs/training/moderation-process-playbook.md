# Moderation Process Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook provides guidance for moderators responsible for reviewing rebuttal submissions before publication on CareHomesSupportDocs.com. It outlines the moderation workflow, review standards, and best practices to help ensure submissions comply with platform policies, privacy requirements, and professional standards.

---

## Audience

This document is intended for:

- Platform Moderators
- Operations Team
- Platform Administrators

---

## Overview

CareHomesSupportDocs.com provides licensed California care facility operators with a platform to publish rebuttals to regulatory citations. Before publication, every rebuttal undergoes a moderation review to help ensure that submitted content complies with platform guidelines and does not contain prohibited or sensitive information.

Moderation focuses on compliance with platform policies and privacy standards. It does **not** verify or determine the factual accuracy of submitted rebuttals.

---

# Moderation Workflow

## Step 1 – Rebuttal Submission

A facility member submits a rebuttal through the member dashboard.

The submission enters the moderation queue for review.

---

## Step 2 – Initial Review

The moderator reviews the submission to ensure:

- Required information has been provided.
- The submission is complete.
- The rebuttal is associated with the correct facility and citation.

---

## Step 3 – Privacy Review

The moderator checks for sensitive or protected information, including:

- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Resident names
- Confidential information prohibited by platform policy

If sensitive information is identified, the submission should be rejected or returned for revision in accordance with moderation guidelines.

---

## Step 4 – Content Review

The moderator reviews the rebuttal for compliance with platform standards.

Review criteria include:

- Professional and respectful language
- Clear and factual statements
- Appropriate formatting
- Compliance with platform policies

Moderators should not provide legal advice or rewrite member submissions.

---

## Step 5 – Moderation Decision

After review, the moderator may:

### Approve

The rebuttal satisfies moderation requirements and may proceed to publication.

### Request Revision

The rebuttal requires changes before publication.

Examples include:

- Privacy concerns
- Missing information
- Formatting issues
- Policy compliance issues

### Reject

The rebuttal cannot be accepted because it violates platform policies.

Reason(s) for rejection should be documented.

---

## Step 6 – Publication

Approved rebuttals are published to the associated facility profile.

> **Status:** Publication workflow for confirmation.

# Moderation Standards

## Privacy Requirements

Moderators must ensure submissions do not disclose information protected by applicable privacy regulations or platform policies.

Examples include:

- Resident medical information
- Resident identities
- Confidential records
- Sensitive personal information

---

## Professional Conduct

Published rebuttals should:

- Remain factual
- Maintain a professional tone
- Avoid inflammatory language
- Avoid defamatory statements

---

## Prohibited Content

The following content should not be published:

- Personal attacks
- Threats or harassment
- Offensive or discriminatory language
- Protected or confidential information
- Content that violates applicable laws or platform policies

# Moderator Responsibilities

Moderators are responsible for:

- Reviewing submitted rebuttals
- Applying moderation standards consistently
- Protecting member and resident privacy
- Documenting moderation decisions
- Escalating complex cases when necessary

---

# Escalation Process

The following situations should be escalated to platform leadership or the designated administrator:

- Suspected legal concerns
- Privacy incidents
- Repeated policy violations
- Technical issues affecting moderation
- Other cases requiring administrative review

---

# Best Practices

Moderators should:

- Apply policies consistently.
- Document moderation decisions clearly.
- Maintain neutrality during review.
- Protect confidential information at all times.
- Escalate uncertain cases rather than making assumptions.

---

# Frequently Encountered Issues

### Submission contains protected information

Return the submission for revision or reject it in accordance with platform policies.

---

### Submission contains inappropriate language

Request revision before publication.

---

### Incorrect facility selected

Return the submission so the member can update the facility information.

---

### Technical issue during review

Document the issue and notify the appropriate support or technical team.
