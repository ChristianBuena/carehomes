# Screencast Plan

## Purpose

This document outlines the planned screencast videos that will be produced for the CareHomesSupportDocs Knowledge Base. These videos are intended to provide quick, task-oriented guidance for client leadership, operations staff, partner attorneys, and facility members during onboarding and ongoing platform use.

---

# Audience

The screencasts are intended for:

- Client Leadership
- Operations Team
- Partner Attorneys
- Facility Administrators
- Platform Members

---

# Knowledge Base

All completed screencasts will be hosted in the CareHomesSupportDocs Notion Knowledge Base.

> **Knowledge Base URL:**  
> `NEXT_PUBLIC_NOTION_KB_URL` (configured in the application environment)

# Recording Guidelines

All screencasts should:

- Be concise and task-focused.
- Use the production user interface whenever possible.
- Demonstrate actual platform workflows.
- Include clear narration.
- Avoid displaying sensitive or confidential information.
- Be updated whenever major platform changes are introduced.

---

# Publication

Completed screencasts will be uploaded to the CareHomesSupportDocs Notion Knowledge Base and made available to authorized users.

Video availability should be reviewed during future platform updates to ensure documentation remains current.

---

# Success Criteria

The screencast library should:

- Include at least five instructional videos.
- Cover the primary user workflows.
- Be accessible through the Knowledge Base.
- Support user onboarding and ongoing platform adoption.
